# Warranty (质保)

- Devices (Echo Dot, Kindle Paperwhite, Fire TV Stick): 1 year limited hardware warranty from the purchase date.
- Smart Home (Ring Doorbell, Smart Plug): 2 year limited hardware warranty.
- Extended protection: 2 additional years can be added within 30 days of purchase for 19.99 USD per device.
- Not covered: accidental damage, water damage on non-waterproof devices, and unauthorized repairs.
- To make a claim, contact support with the order number and a photo of the issue. Approved claims are repaired or replaced within 7 business days.

Devices 类产品质保 1 年，Smart Home 类产品质保 2 年；意外损坏和未授权维修不在质保范围内。
