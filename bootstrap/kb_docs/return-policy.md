# Return and Refund Policy (退货与退款政策)

Applies to all Devices and Smart Home products sold by the Demo Store.

- Return window: 30 days from delivery. Items must be in original packaging with all accessories.
- Refund method: refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item is received.
- Free returns: the first return in any 12 months is free. After that, a flat 4.99 USD return label fee applies.
- Non-returnable: opened Subscription products (Prime Annual, Music Unlimited, Kindle Unlimited) can be cancelled for a prorated refund within 14 days of purchase, but not returned.
- Damaged or defective items: report within 48 hours of delivery to receive a free replacement shipped the same day.

退货窗口为签收后 30 天；退款在收到退货后 5 个工作日内原路退回；12 个月内第一次退货免费。
