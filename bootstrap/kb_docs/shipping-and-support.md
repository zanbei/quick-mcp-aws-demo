# Shipping and Support (配送与客户支持)

## Shipping
- Standard shipping: free for orders over 35 USD, delivered in 3 to 5 business days.
- Express shipping: 9.99 USD, delivered in 1 to 2 business days.
- Regions served: North America, Europe, APAC and China. Delivery times in APAC and China can be 2 days longer.

## How to get help
- Chat support: available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week in the Demo Store app.
- Phone support: weekdays 9:00 to 18:00 local time. Have your order number ready.
- Email support: reply within one business day.
- Accessibility help: for vision or hearing needs, request a call back and an agent will assist with setup.
