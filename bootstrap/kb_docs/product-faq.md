# Product FAQ (产品常见问题)

## Echo Dot
A compact smart speaker with voice control. Setup takes about 5 minutes with the companion app. It supports Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth. It works as a hub for Smart Plug devices.

## Kindle Paperwhite
An e-reader with a 6.8 inch glare-free display and adjustable warm light. Battery lasts up to 10 weeks. It is waterproof (IPX8) and holds about 8 GB of books. It includes accessibility features for low vision readers: adjustable font size and bold text, and VoiceView screen reader over Bluetooth.

## Fire TV Stick
A streaming stick that plays 4K video. It includes a voice remote. It needs an HDMI port and a USB power source.

## Ring Doorbell
A video doorbell with 1080p HD video, motion alerts and two-way talk. It runs on a rechargeable battery or can be wired to existing doorbell wiring. Video history requires the optional Ring Protect plan.

## Smart Plug
A Wi-Fi smart plug that turns a normal appliance on or off by voice or on a schedule. Maximum load is 15 A. It works with Echo Dot.

Echo Dot 支持语音控制，可作为智能插座的控制中心；Kindle Paperwhite 防水，续航最长 10 周。
